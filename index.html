Privacy Policy – Crumbs & Coins

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Crumbs & Coins (“the game”) is an offline idle game for Android.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not need an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, levels, settings) is saved only on your device. If you use Android’s device backup, your progress may be included in your own backup, which is managed by you and your backup provider. You can delete all game data at any time with Settings → Reset all progress in the game, or by uninstalling it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if in-app purchases are added), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/ghorbelhamdi/crumbs-and-coins-privacy.
